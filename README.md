# FIREOUT ONLINE

Play as Elijer, a masked hacker, in the night city of Kagehara. Hack the city with **EX OS HACKERIA**.

Developed by EX ROMANIA. Published by EX Inc.

©2026 EX ROMANIA/EX INC.

Windows: installer (`FIREOUT-ONLINE-Setup-1.0.0.exe`, per-user, no admin) and a portable zip. Use **Check for updates** in the main menu.

## Play

- Skippable boot: REX ENGINE logo, then the EX ROMANIA splash, then the title screen

- Third-person on foot and in cars, same camera as the rest of the FIREOUT line
- **Hold E** to hack a highlighted device through EX OS HACKERIA (traffic signals, street blockers, steam pipes, security cameras, road barriers, YENLINK kiosks)
- The phone opens EX OS HACKERIA, night contracts from Sable, and the skill tree (**Q**)
- Profiler reads a short bio and income on pedestrians you look at
- Heat and a search circle: lose the sweep, take cover with **G**, or drop a city blackout (**B**, once unlocked)
- All names, networks and places are original fiction
